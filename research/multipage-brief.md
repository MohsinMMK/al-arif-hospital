## Motion thesis
One authored care-journey sequence connects three steps with a locally authored Lottie line and advancing dot. GSAP ScrollTrigger scrubs the playhead; the text remains static and readable. GSAP page entries use a short chapter-arrival (horizontal movement with an opacity transition), preserving the header and avoiding a full-screen loading interlude. Context cleanup prevents duplicate triggers on repeated navigation. User motion toggle and OS reduced-motion preference remove spatial travel and show the completed line. No scroll hijacking, auto-looping backgrounds, fake clinical activity or forced waiting.
